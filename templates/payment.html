Privacy, by design

Important
  • No accounts, no tracking profiles. We don’t require email, phone, or social login.
  • No permanent storage. Video and chat never land on our servers. Streams are peer-to-peer.
  • Client-side blur. The adult-content blur filter runs in your browser. We never analyze or log the video frames.
  • Disposable rooms. Every session is temporary. Leave the page and the room ceases to exist.
  • Minimal metadata. We only keep what’s needed to match you with a partner (connection timestamps, rough region). Nothing is tied to your identity.
  • You stay in control. Mute, skip, report, or leave at any moment. Blocked users cannot reconnect to you in the same session.

VeilChat is built for adults who want private, consensual conversations. Please respect others and local laws. We do not tolerate harassment, non-consensual content, or illegal activity.
